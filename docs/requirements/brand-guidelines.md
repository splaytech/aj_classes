## 2. Brand mindset

### What the brand stands for

- **Every student can rise.** Students grow with the right guidance at the right time. Rising is the core idea, and the logo shows it.
- **Learning for everyone.** Hardship shouldn't stop a child learning. This shows up as warmth and inclusion, never as charity marketing.
- **Real attention.** In small groups every student is seen, heard and challenged.
- **Whole-journey support.** Exam *and* interview. Schoolwork *and* exams (aligned to the Victorian Curriculum). Parents are kept informed.
- **Calm, structured method.** Our four steps are **Assess → Plan → Practise → Review**:
  - *Assess:* "We start by knowing your child."
  - *Plan:* "We build a plan that fits."

### Personality

Warm, encouraging, credible, clear. A trusted local teacher, not a high-pressure coaching factory.
We beat bigger competitors on **clarity and calm**, not on noise. Pages are light, airy and easy to scan.
We avoid dark, dense, "premium-luxury" styling and stacked banners.

### Voice and tone

- **Australian English:** programme, enrol, practise (verb), colour, organisation, Year 9 (capital Y).
- **Short and plain:** at most 3 sentences per paragraph. Card titles run 4–6 words. Card points stay within 2 lines.
- **Confident "we" statements:** "We start by knowing your child." Address parents as "you" and "your child".
- **One call to action:** the primary CTA is always **"Register Interest"** (in a sentence: "Register interest →"). The secondary CTA is **"Ask a Question"**. Don't swap in "Enrol now", "Join the waitlist" or "Sign up" (except on platforms whose button names are fixed).
- **Encouraging, never fearful:** no "don't let your child fall behind" pressure.

### Hard rules (legal and trust; every piece must follow these)

1. **No guaranteed or implied results.** Don't use "guaranteed", "100%", "get your child in" or "top results assured".
2. **Past results always carry the year:** "Our 2026 results". Where space allows, add that past results don't predict future ones.
3. **No student names, photos or faces, anywhere.** Teacher or classroom photos need written consent and must show no students.
4. **No unbuilt or unconfirmed claims.** Leave out fees, venues, opening hours and class sizes beyond those stated here. All of these are [TBC].
5. **Original wording.** Never copy competitors' copy or layouts.

### Approved proof points (true and publishable)

- **Our 2026 results:**
  - **10** selective high school offers for Year 9: Melbourne High School 4, Suzanne Cory High School 4, The Mac.Robertson Girls' High School 1, Nossal High School 1.
  - **5** John Monash Science School offers: 3 for Year 10, 2 for Year 11.
  - **3** private school scholarships.
  - Our students also exceeded expectations in NAPLAN and improved significantly in ICAS.
- 20+ years of teaching experience
- Small groups, twice a week
- WWCC-checked, qualified teachers
- Aligned to the Victorian Curriculum

Use the school names exactly as spelt above.

## 3. Logo

### The mark

**"Embossed arrows · 45° · golden"** (final since 1 Oct 2026): three golden arrows rising at 45°, embossed on a rounded light-gold square tile.

- **Meaning:** inspired by the three lines and the third eye on Shiva's forehead. The three lines stand for **knowledge, practice and guidance**. They rise as arrows, echoing "Every student can rise".
- **Note:** some social docs informally call the mark "three upward pencil shapes". The official description is three arrows.
- **Built from yellow gradients only:** pale lemon `#FFF0A8` / `#FFF27A`, gold `#FFC61A` / `#FFCF3A`, deep amber `#EB9A00` → `#C47600`. It has a warm shadow below-right and a white highlight above-left.

### Lockup (logo + wordmark)

The mark sits to the left of the wordmark **"<b>AJ</b> Classes"**: **AJ** is bold and **Classes** is regular, in Inter, with a gap of about 0.3× the mark height.

| Background | Wordmark colour |
|---|---|
| White / light | Navy `#172554` |
| Navy | White `#F8FAFC` |

The wordmark never wraps: "AJ Classes" always stays on one line.

### Usage rules

- **Profile pictures:** the logo centred, filling about 60% of the width, on a solid **navy `#172554`** square. Export it square with no rounded corners and let the platform crop the circle. Don't put text on it.
- **Clear space:** at least 15% of the mark's width on every side.
- **Flat artwork** (single-colour print, embroidery, small sizes) may use a flat **gold `#F9C62B`** square with the arrows in a darker amber.
- **Favicon sizes** (16 / 32 / 48 px) use the same mark, unchanged.
- **Don't:**
  - recolour the mark blue;
  - rotate or stretch it;
  - add outlines;
  - place it on busy photos;
  - put the gold mark on a mid-yellow background, where it disappears.

### Contrast rules (WCAG AA, enforced on the website by a test)

- ✅ Navy or ink text on white. Blue text or buttons on white. White or gold text on navy (gold on navy is about 10:1). Navy text on gold. White text on blue (large or bold text).
- ❌ **Never gold text on white or light backgrounds** (about 1.6:1, unreadable).
- ❌ **Never blue text on navy.**

### Colour balance

- **Mostly white and light**, with blue for action, navy for weight and gold as the spark.
- Rough guide: 60% white/ice, 25% navy/blue, 10% gold, 5% ink text.
- **Social grids:** rotate tile backgrounds through **navy** (white/gold text), **gold** (navy text) and **blue** (white text). No two neighbouring tiles share a colour.
- **Covers and banners:** a horizontal **navy → blue gradient** (`#172554` → `#2563EB`). Thin gold upward arrow shapes rise from the lower right, echoing the logo.

## 5. Typography

- **Typeface:** **Inter** (Google Fonts) for both headings and body. Fallbacks: system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif. Use one family only, with no serif display fonts.
- **Weights:** Regular 400 (body), Medium 500, Semibold 600 (card titles, buttons), Bold 700 (headlines), and 800 only for the "AJ" in the wordmark on large artwork.

**Web type scale:**

| Style | Size |
|---|---|
| H1 | 34–52 px |
| H2 | 28–38 px |
| H3 | 22 px |
| Large | 20 px |
| Body | **18 px** |
| Small | 15 px |
| Label | 12 px |

- **Line height:** 1.2 for headings, 1.6 for body. Keep lines to about 70 characters at most.
- **Print:** keep the same hierarchy. Headline bold, short; body ≥ 10 pt on A5 flyers.
- **Mobile app:** body ≥ 16 px; tap targets ≥ 48 px.
- **Capitalisation:** headlines use sentence case ("Ready for next year's selective entry exam?"). The CTA "Register Interest" uses title case on buttons.

## 6. Layout and visual language

- **Shape:**
  - Radii: 6 px small, 8 px buttons and inputs, 16 px cards.
  - Pills are fully rounded.
  - The logo tile's radius is about 23% of its width.
- **Shadows:** soft, layered and navy-tinted, never harsh black. Cards: `0 1px 2px` + `0 4px 12px` + `0 16px 32px -12px`, all in `rgba(23,37,84, 0.06–0.12)`.
- **Spacing:** a 4 px base (4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 56, 80). Space is generous. Sections are 56 px apart on mobile and 80 px on desktop.
- **Content width:** 1120 px maximum. Gutters are 16 px on mobile and 32 px on desktop.
- **Icons:** the **Lucide** outline set (16 / 20 / 24 px), in blue or navy. Gold icons only on navy.
- **Imagery:**
  - Flat illustration, type and brand shapes (books, charts, targets, rising arrows).
  - **No stock photos of children.**
  - Real teaching-space photos only when empty of students.
- **Cards:**
  - Equal heights in a row.
  - Title 4–6 words; 2–3 points of one line each.
  - A clear link or CTA at the bottom.
- **Announcement pill:** a gold background with navy text and an optional 🔥, e.g. "🔥 2027 classes start in October: Register interest →".
- **Flyer style** (taken from the website hero slides): an eyebrow label, a bold headline, simple artwork, three short points, and a CTA. Tones are blue, navy or gold.
