AJ Classes — logo concepts: Rising lines series

Inspired by the three lines and the third eye on Shiva's forehead: three lines for knowledge, practice and guidance, leaning forward to suggest upward progress (plain lines or arrows, at 75° or 45°). Brand palette: blue #2563EB · yellow #F9C62B · navy #172554. Each card shows the logo on white, on navy, and at favicon sizes (48 / 32 / 16 px).

Lines · 75° · white

Three solid golden lines leaning forward at 75°, a gentle lean that suggests steady progress. Low contrast on white.
