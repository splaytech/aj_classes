AJ Classes — logo concepts: Trinetra series (dot)

Inspired by the three lines and the third eye on Shiva's forehead: three lines for knowledge, practice and guidance, and a single dot (bindu) in place of the eye, for focus. Brand palette: blue #2563EB · yellow #F9C62B · navy #172554. Each card shows the logo on white, on navy, and at favicon sizes (48 / 32 / 16 px).

Trinetra · Dot

Three upright golden lines on a navy disc. A blue dot sits on the middle line: focus at the centre of knowledge, practice and guidance.
