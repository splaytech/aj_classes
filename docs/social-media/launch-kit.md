### 0.1 Handles (same on every platform, in this order)

| Preference | Handle | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `@ajclasses` | Matches the brand and the domain. Best if free everywhere. |

| 3 | `@ajclassesmelbourne` | Descriptive fallback. It reads as Melbourne-only, which undersells the national online offer, so keep it as a last resort. |

**Display names, in order:** `AJ Classes` → `AJ Classes Melbourne` → `AJ Classes | Tutoring`.
Google Business Profile must use the real-world name only: `AJ Classes`. Adding keywords such as "Best Tutoring Melbourne" gets listings suspended.

**Availability checks (do these on the same day, before creating anything):**
1. Search each handle on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok and LinkedIn. Reserve all of them in one sitting so you end up with an identical set.
2. Facebook usernames need 5 or more characters and **ignore full stops**, so `ajclasses.au` and `ajclassesau` are the same username.
3. Instagram and TikTok allow full stops and underscores. TikTok handles can't end in a full stop. YouTube handles are 3–30 characters.
4. WhatsApp has no public handle. It uses the business name plus the phone number, and the link is `https://wa.me/61469800685`.
5. Search IP Australia's trade mark database and ASIC's business names register for "AJ Classes". This is a sanity check, not legal advice.
6. Create every account with `hello@ajclasses.com.au`, turn on two-factor authentication, and add a second admin so the business isn't locked out.

### 0.2 Visual identity (applies to every image below)

- **Logo:** gold rounded square with three upward pencil shapes.
- **Colours:** blue `#2563eb`, navy `#172554`, gold `#f9c62b`. The in-repo `logo.svg` uses a gold gradient; for flat artwork, a flat `#f9c62b` square is fine.
- **Contrast rules:** white or gold text on navy works (gold on navy is about 10:1). White text on blue is fine for large text. Never put blue text on navy, and never put gold text on white.
- **Profile picture (all platforms):** the logo, centred, filling about 60% of the width on a solid navy `#172554` square. Export a **square** image with no rounded corners and let the platform crop the circle. This keeps at least 15% clear space around the logo after the circular crop. No text.
- **Cover/banner (all platforms):** a navy → blue horizontal gradient. Three thin gold upward pencil-tip shapes rise from the lower right, echoing the logo. In white bold sans-serif, left of centre: **"Every student can rise."** Below it in small gold text: "Small-group tutoring · Melbourne & online". Optional gold pill: "Enrolling now for 2027".
- **No student photos, names or faces anywhere.** Use illustration, type and brand graphics. Photos of teachers or classrooms only with written consent, and with no students visible.
- **Post template:** headline top-left, small logo bottom-right, 80 px margins. Rotate the background colour through navy, gold (navy text) and blue (white text).

### 3.4 Asking parents for reviews, ethically

**Rules that keep this clean:**
- Ask **parents or guardians**, never students.
- Ask **every** family in the same way. Don't pick only the happy ones (review gating breaks Google's policy).
- **No incentives** of any kind: no discounts, free lessons, prizes or draws, and no "we'll do one for you" swaps.
- Never write the review for someone, and never ask for a particular star rating.
- Ask parents **not to include their child's name or personal details** in a public review.
- Reply to every review, positive or negative, without confirming anything about an individual student (e.g. "Thank you for your feedback. Please contact us at hello@ajclasses.com.au so we can talk it through.").
- Don't reuse a review as a student "testimonial" in your own marketing without the reviewer's written consent.
- Use the profile's **"Get more reviews"** link or QR code. Don't collect reviews on a shared business device.

**Message to send, same wording for everyone, by email or WhatsApp** (limit: keep under 600 characters)
```text
Hi [parent's first name], thank you for being part of AJ Classes. If you have a few minutes, we'd really appreciate an honest review on Google. It helps other families find us, and your feedback helps us improve. Please don't include your child's name or personal details. Here's the link: [TBC: Google review link]. Thanks again, and no worries at all if you'd rather not.
```
