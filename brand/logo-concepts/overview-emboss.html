AJ Classes — logo concepts: All-yellow embossed

Inspired by the three lines and the third eye on Shiva's forehead: three lines for knowledge, practice and guidance, as three arrows in bright yellow, embossed on a lighter yellow tile, with everything in yellow gradients. Yellow gradients only, from pale lemon #FFFBE0 to deep amber #C47600. Each card shows the logo on white, on navy, and at favicon sizes (48 / 32 / 16 px).
