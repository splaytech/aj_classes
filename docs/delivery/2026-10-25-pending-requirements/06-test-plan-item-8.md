## Pre-freeze findings (raised while reading; ruled and resolved)

- **P1 — the too_late sweep failed rows that should be skipped.** Found reading `996bfbe`
  (`kick_email_worker` swept every pending row past `send_before`, relevant or not, and the claim's skip
  doesn't run while the cap is full). Independently raised as review-5 finding 1 and **fixed in `b804d1f`**
  (D12). E-102 tests the fix end to end under a real cap and the real kick. No ruling needed.
- **P2 — non-admins opening Admin → Emails send a refused `rpc/admin_email_problems`.** `admin-emails.tsx`'s
  `clientLoader` runs in parallel with `admin-layout.tsx`'s guard, so tutor, student, parent and signed-out
  visitors each fire one refused call (403 `not_admin`, or 401). Pre-existing since item 3 (TSR-item-3 "Not
  covered" 2; the same class as item 7's deferred staff-loader reads). **Ruled a defect; fixed in
  `72ccd1f`.** No A3 exception: E-87 is strict.
- **P3 — a due-date edit after publishing doesn't move the "new assignment" deadline.** `send_before` is
  fixed by the publish trigger. If a tutor moves the due date into the past while the row is still pending
  (cap pressure or an outage), the email still goes, saying "It's due <a past date>", which AC-8.4 ("none
  that's out of date") reads against. If it's moved later, the email can expire early. Low: rows normally
  go within 5 minutes. **Ruled a defect; fixed in `edb4f6f`** (skipped at send time once the due date has
  passed; the deadline itself is unchanged). E-45 is strict.
- **P4 — `test:functions` deletes the seed's two "new assignment" rows for "Word problems set 1".**
  `restoreSeededWork` deletes every outbox row of that assignment, every kind. Harmless (they're sent) but it
  changes the outbox and lowers the 24-hour sent count by 2. Low, test hygiene. **Fixed in `ef66c48`**:
  E-09 now expects all 8 seed rows after E-06.
- **P5 — 03b §8.7 "Load failed: defaults (all on) shown" doesn't match the build.** `NotificationsSection`
  shows a `FormAlert` and no switches (item 3's behaviour, item 3 E-35); the defaults fallback in its
  `useState` can't show. E-76 expects the build's behaviour. Docs-only: correct the 03b row.
