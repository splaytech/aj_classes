AJ Classes — logo concepts: Trinetra series

Inspired by the three lines and the third eye on Shiva's forehead: three lines for knowledge, practice and guidance, and the third eye for insight and focus. Brand palette: blue #2563EB · yellow #F9C62B · navy #172554. Each card shows the logo on white, on navy, and at favicon sizes (48 / 32 / 16 px).

Trinetra

The three lines of the tilak, turned upright into three golden pillars (knowledge, practice, guidance). An open eye across them stands for insight and focus.

Tilak
